Requested crop

The region you want to keep.

Each coordinate is an integer from -9223372036854775808 to 9223372036854775807. Enter decimal digits, with a minus sign for negative values.

Minimum edges are included; maximum edges are excluded. Reversed or zero-size rectangles are empty. Touching an edge alone does not create an overlap.
